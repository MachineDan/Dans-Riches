# Dan's Riches

See all your gold at a glance: hover your backpack and the tooltip lists your characters on this realm and how much gold each one has.

## Features

- **Gold in the backpack tooltip:** hover the backpack button on your bag bar. Under Blizzard's usual
  "Backpack" and free-slots lines you get each of your characters on this realm, richest first, with their gold
  and a **Total** line.
- **Pick who shows up:** the options page lists every character Dan's Riches knows, grouped by realm. Untick a
  character to keep them out of the tooltip.
- **Class colors:** names are colored by class. Turn it off for plain white names.
- **Forget** a deleted or transferred character from the list.
- Characters are added the first time you log in on them, and their gold is updated every time it changes.

## Options

Type **/riches**, or open **Esc → Options → AddOns → Dan's Riches**.

- `/riches reset`: class colors and total back on, every character shown again

## Notes

- Only characters you've logged into since installing the addon are known. Log in once on each alt to add them.
- Made for WoW Forever. No libraries.
